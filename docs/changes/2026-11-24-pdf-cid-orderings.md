Decode Identity-H/V CID fonts that have no `/ToUnicode` and no TrueType program (macOS Quartz CJK output) through the registered Adobe character collection named by `CIDSystemInfo` (Adobe-Japan1, GB1, CNS1, Korea1, KR). The CID→Unicode tables are generated by `scripts/gencidmap` from adobe-type-tools/cmap-resources (BSD-3-Clause, notice in `pkg/archive/cidmaps/LICENSE.md`) at pinned commit `f5cf3bc` with SHA-256-verified `cid2code.txt` inputs, store DEFLATE-compressed run-length data (≈150 KiB on disk), and are inflated per ordering on first use. All UTF-32 columns are merged so JIS2004 variant CIDs decode, radicals and compatibility ideographs lose to unified ideographs, and private-use-only CIDs stay dropped. Stripped darwin/arm64 binary grows by 198,864 bytes (+0.30%). Real Quartz fixtures `quartz-hant.pdf` (Japan1 variant CID 20282) and `quartz-ko.pdf` (Korea1) join `quartz.pdf` (#939).
