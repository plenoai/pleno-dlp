Owners: core-engineer, qa

Deepen structured-file extraction in `pkg/archive`. EGG: LZMA blocks (ALZip's 4-byte SDK header and properties), solid archives cut into members by file length, the first volume of a split archive (complete members plus the decodable prefix of the member cut at the volume end), and dummy/skip headers; AZO stays an `unsupported-compression` gap because ESTsoft's reference source forbids its use in other EGG implementations, and split continuation volumes are `split-volume` gaps (#924). PDF: LZW, ASCII85, ASCIIHex, RunLength, and PNG/TIFF predictors; simple fonts decoded through `/Encoding` with `/Differences` glyph names (Adobe Glyph List for New Fonts, BSD-3, embedded as `aglfn.txt`); Type0 fonts with an embedded TrueType cmap; page resources inherited from the `/Pages` tree; form XObjects rendered with their own resources; embedded font programs, images, ICC profiles, and xref streams no longer emitted as text; CID fonts with no Unicode mapping dropped instead of emitted as glyph-index bytes (#925). OLE: `\x01Ole10Native` payloads unwrapped and walked recursively, summary-information property strings decoded, Word footnotes/headers/comments/endnotes/text boxes emitted as separate derived entries, the consumed Word table stream and class-metadata streams no longer scanned raw, and stream names keep the control-character prefix that mscfb strips (the HWP `\x05` check never matched before). `pkg/engine/testdata/benign-office` pins zero findings on a benign legacy Office corpus. Gap kinds are documented in `docs/archive-coverage-gaps.md` (#926).
