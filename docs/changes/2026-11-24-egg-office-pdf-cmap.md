`pkg/archive` now extracts ALZip EGG members (store, raw deflate, and bzip2 blocks, multi-block members, nested archives) through the shared member budget path; encrypted members, AZO/LZMA blocks, and solid/split archives are reported as coverage gaps, and unparseable or solid/split archives fall back to the previous raw scan (closes #890). Legacy binary Office main streams (`WordDocument` piece table, Excel BIFF8 `Workbook` SST/LABEL/STRING, `PowerPoint Document` text atoms) are emitted as document text instead of raw bytes: on a 13-file benign corpus (Word/Excel/PowerPoint 97–2003, including `textutil`-generated man-page documents), #891's raw stream scanning raised findings from 0 to 3 HardcodedPassword false positives from UTF-16-decoded `WordDocument` bytes; with text extraction the corpus yields only the one finding that the same text also yields as plain text. Unparseable streams still fall back to raw scanning and password-protected documents are coverage gaps. PDF page content shown with fonts that carry a `/ToUnicode` CMap (bfchar, bfrange with string or array destinations, codespace ranges, fonts and pages in object streams) is decoded through the CMap; `/ActualText` marked content replaces the glyphs it covers, and same-baseline glyph positioning no longer splits tokens.
