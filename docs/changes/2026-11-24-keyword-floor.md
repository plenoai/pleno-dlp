Leave FullChunkDetector keywords out of the engine's Aho-Corasick prefilter, because `dispatchAt` never dispatches those detectors from a hit. The PII detectors' `0`–`9`, `-`, and `@` keywords had produced a hit on nearly every byte. Add `TestKeywordFloor`, which rejects any window-detector or format keyword shorter than 3 bytes unless `shortKeywordReasons` documents why the detector's regexp has no longer required literal. Six keywords are documented this way: Twilio `AC`, TwilioAPIKey `SK`, Plivo `MA`/`SA`, Bitwarden `0.`, and ZAI `.`. For each of them the keyword already is the longest literal the regexp syntax tree requires, so #1015's proposal to lengthen them has no sound target. On django, dropping every keyword of 2 bytes or fewer, which is unsound, would save at most 1.8 of 44 CPU-s. Re-measuring #1014's whole-blob gate showed it loses: even with every detector that has a keyword of 3 bytes or fewer excluded from the gate (also unsound), it skips 10% of django blobs and the in-process renderer costs 53 CPU-s, against 44 CPU-s for `git log -p`. The gate is therefore not applied. Findings are identical on croc/django/rails git and filesystem scans (#1015).
