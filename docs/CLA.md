## How to sign

The CLA is enforced automatically on pull requests by the CLA bot. When
you open your first PR, the bot comments with a link and a one-line
instruction; you sign by replying with the sentence it quotes. Your
signature is recorded so you only sign once.

Until the bot is activated (see the repository setup notes), maintainers
may ask you to confirm agreement in the PR thread instead.
